Virux-AUv3 Release Notes

Versions 0.1.124 – 0.1.125

v0.1.125 2026-11-08

  • macOS: Virux now recovers after your Mac wakes from sleep. After a wake, the synth's audio could stop arriving while macOS still listed the device, so Virux never noticed and the synth stayed silent until you reconnected it. Virux now notices when the synth's audio stops arriving for about 1.5 seconds and runs its usual recovery, and its recovery timers now count the time the Mac was asleep. A DAW that is paused or stops taking audio is not mistaken for a lost synth.
  • macOS: the standalone app reconnects to your synth after the Mac wakes from sleep. Sleep could break the connection to the synth while it still appeared in the device list. The app now reopens the synth you selected, and if the synth is not ready yet it tries again up to three times, five seconds apart. After a wake or a replug it reconnects only the synth you selected, never another attached synth, and never after you have pressed Disconnect.
  • Editor: choosing Multi or Sequencer as the play mode now puts the synth in that mode. Previously the two were swapped on the TI, so selecting Multi put the synth in Sequencer mode and the other way round. Projects keep the mode you chose, and an invalid saved mode falls back to Multi. Single mode is still never selected, because it cannot return audio over USB.
  • Testing: new coverage for the play mode sent to the synth for each Multi and Sequencer choice, including the fallback to Multi. The macOS audio stall check is tested for a synth that stops sending audio after a wake, for a paused DAW, which must not count as a stall, and for a fresh start after each restart.
